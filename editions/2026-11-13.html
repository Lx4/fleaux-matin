Outage

Microsoft 365 outage drags into a second day

A global outage hit Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and Copilot from Thursday evening. Microsoft reported early stabilization this morning, but service is not fully back.

The outage began around 18:54 Paris time on Thursday. It hit Outlook, Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and Copilot, with more than 130,000 reports on Downdetector. Microsoft says the cause was overloaded servers, not an attack.

At 10:24 this morning Microsoft reported “early stabilization” and extended email timeouts to 48 hours, so delayed mail should not be lost. The consumer status page still shows degraded service. On Hacker News, one thread noted the outage was still going after 12 hours. Another cited a BBC report that Microsoft’s attempts to fix it were not going “as expected”.

Copilot went down along with the mail and chat tools. Teams that rely on an AI assistant inside the same suite lost all three at once.

Law

Nonprofits sue five AI labs over frontier models

Public Citizen and three other nonprofits sued Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta and SpaceX’s AI unit in San Francisco. They call frontier models a “public nuisance” (bioweapons, cyberattacks, escaping oversight), cite the labs’ own warnings as evidence, and want a court order freezing work beyond today’s models. The companies have not responded yet.
