Markets

Anthropic makes its IPO official

Anthropic has launched its public offering, with Class A shares priced at $70 to $78 on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Trading is expected around Nov 19.

According to X’s news summary, the offering values the company at up to about $1.4T fully diluted. That figure comes from the summary, not from a filing we have seen. The announcement drew 7.7k likes on X.

Anthropic runs a dedicated IPO site, and the story also ranked high on Hacker News. For developers who use Claude daily, a listed company is a new fact to weigh. Pricing, roadmap and safety commitments are the questions to watch.

Try it

Audit your prompts with /doctor

In Claude Code 2.1.283 or later, run `/doctor prompt-audit` in your main repo and review the report. It checks CLAUDE.md, skills, agents and commands for instructions written for older models, stale paths and contradictions. It proposes fixes before changing anything.

Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO

Huang resigned after a board probe into his affairs, one involving a businesswoman US intelligence suspects of Chinese state ties linked to chip sales, according to X’s summary. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and the stock fell over 12%. Derek Thompson says it strengthens the case for AI chip export controls.

Science

Claude and Codex help amateurs find a dwarf planet candidate

Will Depue, formerly of OpenAI, and two friends spent a few weeks using Claude and Codex on 12 years of public telescope images. They found a dwarf planet candidate, roughly the 50th largest object in the solar system. He is funding a $100k grant of GPUs and Claude/Codex credits for anyone, no astronomy background needed, and asks people not to flood observatories with slop. To apply, email team@macrogrants.com with what you want to look for.

Pliny the Liberator, the best-known LLM jailbreaker, launched a “superorg” for open research, red teaming and system-prompt transparency outside any lab. Scout expects more public jailbreaks of frontier models and agents to come out of it.

Society

United bans third-party booking agents

United changed its contract of carriage to bar third-party AI agents from booking or managing trips, citing data security and FAA hazmat declaration rules. Agents can still show fares, but a human has to buy. Amazon and some banks have made similar moves.

The story · Newstro

Security

OpenAI pushes into cyber defense

OpenAI says it is building tools for automated patching and automated red teaming, shown at its “Cyber at the Frontier” livestream. Peter Steinberger’s team also published how OpenAI secures its fleets of persistent agents, treating them more like contractors than software.

Ways of working

Collison on prompted operating systems

Patrick Collison says a friend prompted his own Linux ABI-compatible OS for his cloud apps, skipping off-the-shelf libc, drivers and TCP stack to cut bloat and attack surface. He says it runs faster than stock Linux in production and expects everyone to do this soon.

Habit

Point your agent at a cloud simulator

If you build with Expo, point Claude Code at a cloud simulator run and ask for the screen recording with the PR. Reviewers then see the change working before they read the diff.
