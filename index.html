Claude beats quantum baselines with a scoring loop

Anthropic gave Claude eight quantum circuit-design problems and an automated evaluator. Iterating against it, the model beat published baselines, including a decoder with 12x fewer gates.

The setup was simple. Each problem came with an evaluator that checks correctness and cost. Claude proposed designs, got scored, and tried again.

The reported result: better than published baselines on the problems tested, among them a decoder using 12x fewer gates. The win is credited to the closed loop with a checker, not to a cleverer prompt.

The lesson carries over to everyday code. A script that scores output, say tests plus a performance or size number, gives an agent something firm to iterate against.

Give your agent a scorer
